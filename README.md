# 7-Card-Game (7 Cards Least)

🎮 **Play Live Online**: [https://cards.gnanamai.com/](https://cards.gnanamai.com/)

A fast-paced online multiplayer card shedding game where the goal is to finish with the lowest cumulative score. Play solo against computer AI or create private multiplayer rooms to play with friends and family with no download required.

### 🃏 Rules & Gameplay
- Each player receives 7 cards.
- The remaining cards form a face-down draw pile, with one card turned face-up as the discard pile.
- Discard high-value cards, form matching rank sets or suited runs, and declare **LEAST!** when your hand total drops to 10 points or less.
- Learn more at [7 Cards Least Rules & Strategy](https://cards.gnanamai.com/7-cards-least/rules).
